Hyperplace

Hyperplace is an open, self-sovereign spatial identity primitive. Place is a pattern, not a coordinate — the missing layer is sovereignty, not better mapping.

A place can be registered on-chain with a sovereign owner, resolved by a permanent id, and updated only by that owner. Hyperplace is a commons to build on, not a product to sign up for.
